Get Involved

Three ways to move this forward.

Whether you have an hour a week, a classroom, or a budget line, there is a role that fits.

Contribute time

Volunteer

Volunteers run outreach, translate materials, coordinate camps, facilitate peer groups, and lead destigmatisation sessions. Active chapters exist across 22 countries including India, the UK, the US, the UAE, Singapore, and Canada.

Onboarding includes an application, a short interview, a safeguarding briefing, and pairing with an existing chapter lead. Vetting is mandatory for any role that involves contact with students.

Apply to volunteer →
Work with us

Partner With Us

We partner with schools, NGOs, and clinics that share our commitment to inclusion. A school partnership typically covers destigmatisation sessions, teacher orientation, and hosting a diagnostic camp for identified students.

The commitment: a named school coordinator, access to student cohorts for structured sessions, and follow-through on classroom accommodations after diagnosis.

Enquire about partnering →
Fund a camp

Fund a Camp

Funding covers psychiatrist fees, on-site logistics, printed diagnostic materials, family follow-up communication, and post-camp school liaison. Volunteer time is not billed.

Because we work through existing school partnerships and a volunteer network, the per-student cost of a diagnostic assessment is a fraction of what a private clinic charges. We publish a full cost breakdown per camp on request.
