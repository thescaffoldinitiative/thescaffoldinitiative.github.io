Programmes

Three programmes. One continuous pathway.

Each programme is designed to close a specific gap. Together they form the pipeline from a student who doesn't know why school is hard, to a student who has a plan.

01
Diagnostic Camps

Diagnostic Camps

Free diagnostic camps with licensed psychiatrists, targeting students in underserved regions where private diagnosis is inaccessible or unaffordable.

Referrals come through partner schools and community organisations. Families receive a written diagnosis where applicable, a clinical summary they can share with the school, and a suggested next-step pathway including local resources.

A single camp typically assesses 20–40 students over one to two days.

02
School Partnerships

School Partnerships

A partnership involves destigmatisation sessions with student cohorts, teacher orientation on classroom accommodations, and ongoing classroom culture work.

Current reach: 15+ schools across Delhi NCR, Chandigarh, and Jaipur, with expansion planned into additional tier 2 cities.

Schools commit to a structured multi-month engagement rather than one-off talks, that's what actually shifts culture.

03
Post-Diagnostic Support

Post-Diagnostic Support

Peer-led support groups, helpline facilitation, and school liaison to make sure a diagnosis actually translates into academic accommodation.

Most organisations stop at diagnosis. This phase exists precisely because a diagnosis without follow-through changes very little, the student still walks back into the same classroom on Monday.

We stay involved for at least the academic year following diagnosis.
